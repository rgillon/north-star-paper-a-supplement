# A genuine pair with 196 squarefree odd factors

**Proved.** There is a squarefree odd integer M with 196 prime factors for which the two subgroup unions specified below in the cyclic group of order 2^3214 M are homometric at every labeled shift and are translation-inequivalent. In particular some divisibility-minimal nonrigid modulus divides this order. Squarefree existence was already known from PPI-CE1.

Use the [exact certificate](../experiments/level1_squarefree_star_pair.py) for the ordered prime lists and rational parameters. Start with the four fixed blocks of [WF1](PPI_WEIGHTED_FIBER_PAIR.md), and apply the partial upper extension of [UP2](PPI_UNBALANCED_PAIR.md) twice, with prime pairs (1301,1303) and (179,181). This gives 52 distinct fixed primes and combined rank bound 48. UP2 supplies both the corrected upper witness and the moving correction in the presence of a nonzero local mean. Keep its independent upper helper.

On three fresh coordinates p,q,r with r=p+q−1, use discrepancy +1 on each of the singleton order shells p,q and −1 on shell r, zero elsewhere. Its sum is (p−1)+(q−1)−(r−1)=0. Its support is an antichain, so its lower and upper closures intersect exactly on the support. If their indicators are D and C, the local rank 1−D+C is increasing and takes values 0,1,2. Comparable orders of equal rank preserve both indicators and their product. The constant one half annihilates this discrepancy under convolution and approximates each of D,C,DC within one half. These are precisely the damping-block inputs to the WF1 interpolation, including the moving-rank terms, where a removed damping coordinate costs one factor of two. Use the first 34 disjoint triples of the certificate scan. This gives rank bound 116. All tensor annihilation identities hold at individual characters since each summand has an annihilating coordinate.

For the spectator use the seven raw tags and eighteen compensated tags generated by the certificate. The first four compensated tags are the guards. Use the WF1 ordinary slots of size 22 missing at least one guard and the fine slots consisting of the guards and seven of the other fourteen compensated tags. Include their strict divisors as in WF1. The product B of odd parts of compensated predecessors is squarefree on 17 fresh primes, by trial factorization. On a fine slot of weight V the subgroup of order B/oddpart(V) and the dyadic adjustment of WF1 give the same binary unit for every slot. The maximum extra dyadic exponent is two. These restrictions define ideals since strict subtags used as background are full.

Apply the WF1 rational profile and paired-anchor blend, with the fixed bounds updated by UP2. Use the certificate's first 700 ordinary slots and split after 320. Its exact arithmetic verifies the WF1 common interval for all ranks, the step-grid exponent 147, ordinary depth 171, and binary precision A=3212. The binary slots fit since 3212+116 ≤ 3432. The greedy remainder argument in WF1 applies to each interior value on the certified grid, using the group sum and individual increment bounds checked by verify_templates. Thus each prescribed free profile value is realized by the WF1 decoding rule with nested sections, at height 3212+2. This checks the hypotheses of the WF1 lifting argument with the new blocks. Specifically, the two indicators differ by the spectator-constant product discrepancy on the boundary, and their sum has spectator average equal to the annihilating profile. Convolving difference with sum gives zero at every shift (both indicators are even). At the spectator origin the two odd-core sections differ. A translation between two even sets gives a period equal to twice the translation, so its odd component is itself a period. On a differing boundary fiber each section is full or empty throughout the spectator, excluding a translation on the remaining coordinates. Counting axes gives 52+25+17+3·34=196.

**Proved minimality alternative.** Let L be the product of 2^3214 and the 94 fixed donor and spectator primes above. Either L is nonrigid or there are infinitely many divisibility-minimal nonrigid moduli with squarefree odd part, each having at most 230 odd prime factors and dyadic exponent at most 3214.

Indeed, any finite list of excluded primes can be avoided by four fresh primes a<b<c<d with b−a=d−c. Pair consecutive allowed primes up to X in disjoint adjacent pairs. If all their positive gaps are distinct their sum grows at least quadratically in the number of pairs, yet is at most X. For large X the prime number theorem contradicts this bound. Use signs + on a,d and − on b,c. The same balance, rank and half-error proof applies. Replace all 34 triples by such quartets, repeatedly avoiding all previously used damping primes. The resulting orders have pairwise gcd L. Every nonrigid order has a divisibility-minimal nonrigid divisor. If L is rigid no two orders can share such a divisor, since a pair lifts to any multiple by embedding its subgroup unions in the unique subgroup of the original order (shifts outside have zero overlap, and a translating element must belong to that subgroup). This proves the alternative.

**Gap.** The construction gives no rigidity decision for L and does not identify which divisors of the explicit example are minimal.
